Thank you for the review. We retested this same build on a clean device from a cold launch. We could not reproduce a crash, a dead end, an empty screen, or a non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate. Each can look like a stall or a dead control if the designed condition is missed.

1. After a successful Press, Leaves opens with the new tile at the top and today locks. The control stays dimmed with "This leaf is pressed." Close or a swipe down returns to that same locked leaf. The halves will not take new ink.

2. A day that had only one filled half returns under "Carried over", then that person's name and "already sketched. The other half is still open." The filled half is dimmed; the other half still takes ink. Draw there, then Press.

3. On Pressed, Share is visible but disabled until the PNG is ready. When the picture is ready, Share opens the system share sheet. That wait is preparation, not a broken control.
